In the creaking belly of Ryze's imposing tower-heart, you roll restlessly in your meager cot. Nauseous with anger, fear, and confusion.

The machine, the pipe-work image of the divinity of man, Giriam has a single function.

A synthetic god.

And all this time, all your days as a *dog* you have been enacting a single purpose.

Compensate where the machine fails.

These mages have created a flawed, but monumental dominion over fate. They own the future. And you are the patchwork solution for their greatest weakness. Killing any force that could harm its design.

Tserlbane must die.
